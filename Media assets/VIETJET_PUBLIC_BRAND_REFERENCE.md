# VietJet Air Public Brand Reference Notes

**Important:** This is an unofficial research note for visual reference, not an official VietJet Air brand standards document. I could not verify a publicly downloadable, official, complete VietJet brand guideline/manual. Do not describe these notes as official specifications.

For the VietJet PTFS project, use the real airline only as a visual reference. VietJet PTFS is an independent Roblox virtual-airline community, not VietJet Air. Keep the community's own logo, name, and disclaimer clear, and do not imply endorsement or affiliation.

## 1. What official public sources confirm

- **Core livery colors:** red and yellow. VietJet's official fleet page states that its aircraft designs refer to the national colors, red and yellow.
- **Brand values:** the official company page lists Safety, Happiness, Affordable Price, and Punctuality as core values.
- **Personality/culture cues:** the official company page describes safety awareness, integrity, differentiation and inspiration, diligence and resourcefulness, joyfulness, strength, and excitement.
- **Aircraft visual language:** the airline's public fleet/news pages show a strong red fuselage, yellow tail accents/details, white lettering, and occasional special aircraft graphics promoting destinations, tourism, or partnerships.

Official references:

## 5. Practical visual direction for VietJet PTFS

Recommended interpretation for a separate virtual airline:
- Use red as the main brand accent and yellow for highlights, route markers, small accent strokes, and important status indicators.
- Keep most website surfaces white or neutral/dark so the red and yellow remain readable; avoid filling every panel with saturated red.
- Use clear high-contrast typography, restrained shadows, consistent spacing, and a responsive layout.
- Prefer original PTFS screenshots: aircraft at gate, takeoff/approach, taxiing, cockpit/ground operations, and group flights.
- Use energetic aviation photography and Vietnam/destination imagery where appropriate, but do not imply that real aircraft or official VietJet Air services are part of the Roblox community.
- Voice recommendation inferred from published corporate values: friendly, upbeat, clear, safety-conscious, and travel-focused. This is a design recommendation, not an official voice/tone rule.
