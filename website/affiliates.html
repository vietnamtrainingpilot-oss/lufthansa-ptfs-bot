Affiliates and partners

We fly alongside other Roblox aviation communities. If your community runs a virtual airline and meets the conditions below, we would like to hear from you.

Start a partnership enquiry

What we look for

Eight conditions, agreed up front, so both sides know what a partnership looks like.

An active community

Your server should have a real, participating membership rather than an empty roster.

A published schedule

Flights should be scheduled in advance so that joint operations can be planned.

Staffed operations

Each flight should have a host or enough crew to operate it safely.

Conduct rules

A written code of conduct covering behaviour, harassment and moderation.

No real-money trading

Points, credits or ranks must not be sold, traded or cashed out for real currency or Robux.

Responsive staff

Someone who can coordinate a joint event when it is arranged.

Clear branding rights

Both sides agree in writing how logos and names may be used.

An honest description

Your community should describe itself accurately, including that it is unofficial.

What partners get

  • Coordinated joint flights and occasional shared events.
  • A dedicated contact in our staff team for planning.
  • Cross-promotion between communities, subject to both sides agreeing.
  • A partner role in our server where applicable.

Apply to partner

Open a partnership ticket and include:

  • Your community name and a link to it
  • Server member count, excluding bots
  • Your route network and fleet
  • Reputation within the Roblox aviation community
  • Any previous joint operations
