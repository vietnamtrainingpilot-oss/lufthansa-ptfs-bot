Every department keeps the network flying. Applications are reviewed by staff in our Discord server and are open year-round.

How to apply See the routes

Find your place in the operation

Six departments, each with its own role, training path and responsibilities.

How the application works

Applications are completed entirely in Discord so that staff can review and record them consistently.

Join the Discord server

Everything happens in the community server. Make sure your Discord profile is filled in and readable.

Open the careers menu

Use the /careersetup menu posted in the careers channel and choose your department.

Read and accept the agreement

You will receive the application agreement in a direct message. You must accept it to continue.

Answer the questions

Each question has a ten minute timer. Your answers go directly to the reviewing staff member.

Await the decision

Staff typically respond within 48 to 72 hours. Results are sent to you privately and announced publicly.

Do not use artificial intelligence to write your answers. Using AI in an application is grounds for an immediate denial. Write in your own words.
