Flights and booking

Every departure below is published in the live schedule. Request a seat with your Discord account, fly the route, and staff will confirm your attendance before any rewards are issued.

How booking works

Submit your seat request

Pick a cabin and enter your Discord user ID. Your request is written to the flight manifest.

Check in on the day

Check-in opens at the scheduled departure time. Be at the gate before departure.

Staff confirm attendance

After the flight, authorised staff mark each passenger present, no-show or removed.

Rewards are issued once

Only confirmed attendees on a completed flight receive points and credits, exactly once.

Good to know

Booking does not earn anything. Rewards depend on verified participation, not on holding a seat.

No-shows earn nothing. The manifest is the record of who actually flew.

Cancelled flights earn nothing. If a flight is cancelled you simply do not receive a reward.

Delays do not disqualify you. A delayed flight still earns once it is completed.

Need help? Open a ticket through the community Discord and staff will assist.
