Four steps to your first flight

Everything runs through the Discord server, and every step is auditable by staff.

1

Join the Discord

The community server is where flights are announced, staff coordinate, and your account lives.

2

Pick a departure

Browse the published schedule and request a seat in your cabin class.

3

Fly and check in

Show up on time. Staff confirm attendance on the flight manifest before anything is awarded.

4

Collect your rewards

Once the flight is completed, points and credits are added to your account exactly once.

Two balances, earned by flying

Loyalty points recognise how much you fly. LH Credits are a separate closed virtual economy you spend on perks. Neither balance has cash value, and neither can be transferred or bought.
