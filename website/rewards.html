Fair play

The economy is designed so that flying more genuinely earns more, and so that it cannot be gamed.

  • Every balance change is written to an append-only ledger entry in the same database transaction.
  • Each passenger can be awarded exactly once per flight. Re-running the award command, restarting the bot, or a duplicated request will not pay twice.
  • Staff corrections create compensating entries. History is never erased.
  • Purchases use a database transaction, so a double click or a retry cannot spend the same balance twice or oversell a limited item.
  • There are no gambling mechanics, no paid random rewards, no real-money purchases and no Robux purchases.
  • Points and credits cannot be transferred between members.
