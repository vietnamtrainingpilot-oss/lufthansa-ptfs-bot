Support and policies

All support is handled through our Discord helpdesk so that staff can record and track every request.

Open the Discord Track a booking

Getting help

Use the helpdesk menu in our Discord server. It creates a private ticket channel with you and the staff member handling it, so your request is never lost in public chat.

  • General — general questions and assistance
  • Partnership — affiliate and partnership enquiries
  • Reports — report a member or an issue
  • Fast Pass — direct staff entry requests
  • Other — anything else

Bookings

A seat request records you on the flight manifest with a status of not confirmed. That status changes to present only when staff confirm your attendance after the flight.

  • Requesting a seat does not reserve a specific seat number. Any preference you enter is a request only.
  • Check-in opens at the scheduled departure time and closes at departure.
  • If you cannot fly, tell staff so your manifest entry can be corrected.
  • Rewards are issued only for confirmed attendance on a completed flight, and only once.

Careers applications

Applications are completed entirely in Discord. Use the careers menu in the careers channel, accept the agreement, and answer each question within its ten minute window.

  • Applications that time out are closed automatically and can be restarted.
  • Using artificial intelligence to write answers results in an immediate denial.
  • Results are sent privately and announced in the public results channel.

Partnership enquiries

Open a partnership ticket and include your community name, invite link, member count excluding bots, your route network, and any previous joint operations. Staff review enquiries on a case-by-case basis.

Privacy

Signing in to this site uses Discord OAuth. We request only the identify permission, which gives us your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar.

We do not request your email address, and we never ask for or store your Discord password. Your session is a signed cookie held in your browser and expires automatically.

Your loyalty record is stored server-side and is only ever returned to you. Staff adjustments are recorded in an internal audit log that you cannot read, and you cannot read another member's data by changing a URL or an API request.

To have your record reviewed or corrected, open a helpdesk ticket.
